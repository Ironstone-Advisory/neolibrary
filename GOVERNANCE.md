*By Rob Tyrie, Ironstone Advisory. Licensed [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-docs).*

NeoLibrary is built with Rob Tyrie's creative flow (ideate, think, make, do, judge, decide, execute, archive), with a skeptic that can be called into any part of it. One rule is fixed: **nothing goes public until the Bullshit Compass has run on it at least once.**

The full rule is [RFC-0004: How NeoLibrary is built](rfcs/RFC-0004-how-we-build.md). Decisions about what NeoLibrary *is* live in the other RFCs; the [RFC index](rfcs/README.md) lists them.

Ironstone Advisory owns the project and Rob Tyrie has the final decision on what is accepted.
