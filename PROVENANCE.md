# Provenance

This file records where NeoLibrary came from, using dated primary sources. Anything not backed by a primary source is marked as such.

**Owner:** Ironstone Advisory, wholly owned by Rob Tyrie. Ironstone Advisory operates as a numbered company; the registered name will be added after counsel's review.
**Author of the concept and the name:** Rob Tyrie.

## How to read this record

- **Primary source**: an email, file or message with a system timestamp (Gmail, Google Drive, WhatsApp).
- **Recollection**: Rob's account, not yet tied to a timestamped source.
- **AI-assisted**: a document drafted with an AI tool from Rob's prompts and direction. These are labelled. Rob is the author of the direction and the decisions; the tool produced the draft text.

Evidence copies are held by Ironstone Advisory in a private archive (`neolibrary-provenance`), indexed with SHA-256 hashes so any copy can be checked against its original.

## Three phases

| Phase | Dates | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| Before Astra | to May 15, 2024 | Rob works on knowledge-base problems for Ironstone and for the Grey Swan Guild, then conceives and names NeoLibrary |
| Astra | May to about Oct 2024 | A bounded, Guild-branded grant phase ("Astra" / "Ad Astra"). NeoLibrary was the subject of an AWS Imagine grant application. The grant was not awarded and the phase ended |
| After Astra | 2025 onward | Rob continues development independently under Ironstone Advisory: DAC-EK, the Neo Librarian, the Helix |

## What this record does and does not claim

- It claims authorship of the concept, the name and its continued development by Rob Tyrie, and ownership by Ironstone Advisory.
- It does not claim the Grey Swan Guild corpus. That content belongs to its authors and is used under CC BY 4.0 as test data in `neolibrary-commons`.
- It does not claim Patrick McDonald's separate data-first version.
- It does not claim the AdaptiveGaming exercise (Sept 2024), which is group work under CC BY 4.0, hosted as a courtesy archive at `Ironstone-Advisory/adaptivegaming`. No AdaptiveGaming material is in this repo.
- No evidence before February 2024 has been located yet. If earlier sources are found they will be added with dates.
